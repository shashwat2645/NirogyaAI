Our AI Prototypes

PrakritiAI

Discover your unique Ayurvedic constitution (Vata, Pitta, Kapha, or a mix). PrakritiAI asks simple lifestyle and health questions, then uses AI to map responses to Ayurvedic principles. Your prakriti profile becomes the base for all personalized wellness recommendations.

NidaanAI

Your AI-powered health analyst. NidaanAI interprets your medical reports and symptoms in simple terms. By identifying possible dosha imbalances, it gives insights into underlying issues like stress, acidity, or low energy, bridging the gap between raw data and meaningful health advice.

VivekaAI

A personalized lifestyle and wellness coach. VivekaAI combines your prakriti profile with health insights to suggest diets, yoga, breathing exercises, and meditation practices. It ensures your daily routine stays balanced and aligned with Ayurvedic wisdom for long-term well-being.
