✨ What is Ayurveda?

Ayurveda, literally translated as the "Science of Life," is a 5000-year-old Indian medical system that goes beyond curing diseases—it focuses on maintaining harmony between the body, mind, and soul. Unlike modern medicine which often treats only symptoms, Ayurveda emphasizes prevention and holistic healing through lifestyle, natural herbs, yoga, meditation, and diet.

📜 History of Ayurveda

Ayurveda finds its roots in the Vedas, especially in texts like the Charaka Samhita (focused on internal medicine) and the Sushruta Samhita (known for ancient surgery). For thousands of years, it has guided human civilization in understanding the natural connection between environment and health. Even today, its principles continue to inspire a global movement towards natural and sustainable healthcare.

⚖️ The Three Doshas

Central to Ayurveda is the concept of the doshas—the three fundamental energies governing the body:

  • Vata — linked with movement, air, and creativity.
  • Pitta — linked with digestion, fire, and transformation.
  • Kapha — linked with stability, water, and growth.

Everyone is a unique combination of these doshas. Keeping them balanced ensures energy, immunity, and overall well-being, while imbalance can lead to health issues.
